THE WOMAN WHO KEPT HER HEART

THE PRICE OF LOVE

Inspired by a True Story

LOGLINE

Tolu loves Chinedu and sacrifices comfort, resources and dreams for the relationship, but family expectations, rejection and betrayal force her to discover that losing a relationship does not mean losing herself.

SERIES OVERVIEW

The Woman Who Kept Her Heart is an original Nigerian drama property following Tolu and Chinedu through love, sacrifice, faith, ambition, family expectations and the difficult choices that shape their journey. Across five seasons, the story explores not only the relationship between two people, but also identity, purpose, resilience and healing.

GENRE & FORMAT

Nigerian Drama • Romance • Emotional Storytelling
5 Seasons • 25 Episodes • Continuing Storyline

THE FIVE-SEASON EMOTIONAL JOURNEY

Season 1 — THE BEGINNING
A new relationship begins and two young people discover love, faith and the possibility of a shared future.

Season 2 — BUILDING TOGETHER
Love develops into partnership as Tolu and Chinedu work toward building a life together.

Season 3 — BUILDING THE DREAM
Their ambitions grow, bringing new opportunities, responsibilities and challenges.

Season 4 — THE CRACKS BEGIN
Pressure, expectations and difficult choices begin to test everything they have built.

Season 5 — THE PRICE OF LOVE
The consequences of love and sacrifice unfold, leading to loss, healing and the discovery of a deeper sense of self.

THE CORE

At its heart, the series explores love, sacrifice, faith, identity, rejection, resilience, healing and self-worth.

IP OPPORTUNITY

The Woman Who Kept Her Heart is designed as a long-form Nigerian drama property with potential for screen adaptation, production, distribution, licensing and strategic partnerships.
